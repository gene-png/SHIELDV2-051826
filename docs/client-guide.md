# Client guide

> Stub. Lands alongside §6 (client surface) and §7 (intake wizard).

SHIELD by Kentro helps you see where you stand on cybersecurity. After sign-up and intake, your home dashboard shows what's waiting on you, what your consultant is working on, and the headline findings from each engagement service.

You will:

1. **Sign up** with your work email.
2. **Complete intake** — a 6-step wizard. Pick services, describe your systems, answer the questionnaire(s), upload artifacts.
3. **Watch the home dashboard** — when a deliverable is ready, you'll see the headline immediately.
4. **Read or download** deliverables under `/documents`.
5. **Message your consultant** under `/messages`.

Every page is plain English. We never expose the methodology internals or raw scoring math.
